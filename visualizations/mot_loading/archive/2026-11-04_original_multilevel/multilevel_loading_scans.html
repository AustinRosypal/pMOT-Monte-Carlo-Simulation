Physics, sampling, and comparison

The physical multilevel model resolves 8 ground and 16 excited hyperfine-Zeeman states. ARC supplies dipoles, transition frequencies, pairwise spontaneous rates and Landé factors before trajectory calculations. Stimulated coefficients are W = Γe|Ω|²/(Γe²+4Δ²); saturation arises from coupled populations. Each beam force uses the net absorption-minus-stimulated-emission rate W(pg−pe).

Cooling detuning is relative to F=2 → F′=3. Repump detuning is relative to F=1 → F′=2; zero means on resonance. The six repump components follow the same Cartesian directions as cooling. Their default power is 0.1 mW each and diameter 12.7 mm. The two cooling-diameter studies hold repump diameter fixed or match it to cooling diameter, while holding both powers fixed.

Common scans reuse the exact two-level launch coordinates, disc counts and 50 points per disc. Directions cover the full sphere; launch points are uniform in area on 15 mm-radius discs whose centers are 15 mm from the origin. Velocities on each disc are parallel to the inward normal. The launch aperture and plane remain finite. No extra octant or 4π factor is applied.

Gravity is enabled; recoil diffusion is absent. Fixed RK4 uses the same 5 µs base step, with local fields, intensities, polarization decomposition, populations and force recomputed at each stage. Capture uses 5 ms continuous residence within the 2 mm core OR two entries separated by an exit. Capture boundaries and exceptional direct velocity masks receive the recorded dual-timestep and longer-duration audits.

The two saturation plots are nominal single-beam center quantities using the benchmark I_sat = 1.669 mW/cm² and linewidth 6.065 MHz. They share the same power trajectories. The physical kernel retains ARC-derived state-dependent decay rates and resolved Zeeman/polarization physics; its restoring propagation-frame helicities are sigma+ on x/y and sigma− on z in both directions. These are the physical realization of the two-level model’s effective axis signs.

Intervals describe Monte Carlo uncertainty across independent direction discs, not model error or residual numerical error. A zero sampled mean does not establish a zero physical cross section or a useful relative error bound. The physical model assumes instantaneous local steady-state populations and incoherent beams. Frozen-environment event checks validate its rate kernel, not a recoil trajectory or adiabatic population-relaxation model. Weak-wing population-relaxation limitations remain relevant. No atomic-temperature or equilibrium-temperature claim is made.
